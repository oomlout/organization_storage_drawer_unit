V7 FOUR-FINGER MECHANISM CARD — PETG / nominal 6 mm MDF

Print 3dpr.stl ONCE: FOUR numbered clips (50–53) + ONE separate locking key.
Print receiver_6_0.stl ONCE and screw_test.stl ONCE (beam end + adapter).
Keep supplied flat orientations, labels UP. Use production PETG settings; no designed supports.
sample_*.stl and keeper_key.stl are replacement pieces, not additional kit quantities.

50 — straight cantilever arms: control for insertion and squeeze-to-release.
51 — tapered arms: 1.8 mm at the root to 1.2 mm at the hook; same mating envelope.
52 — S-shaped arms: curved spring paths within the beam; same hook and hole.
53 — snap plus removable blocking key: insert the clip normally, then slide the separate
key down between the hooks from OUTSIDE. The key blocks inward hook movement.
Remove the key before squeezing the hooks. It is an intentionally removable loose key;
captive/anti-loss retention is not developed. It does not provide force-free insertion.
The key is a separate optional lock, not a permanent centre tongue.

All five receiver holes are IDENTICAL 6.0 x 4.6 mm, labelled 50 / 51 / 52 / 53 / M3.
All clips use a 5.5 mm neck, 0.25 mm nominal axial play and matching external hooks.
Each clip has TWO inner latch arms and TWO thicker outer seating strips. There is no
centre tongue. Compare mechanisms first; do not sand/tune individual fits during the comparison.
Insert clips from the UNLABELLED inside; hooks emerge on the LABELLED outside.
Record effort, release, play, repeated use and damage in results.csv; regeneration preserves it.

LASER: receiver_laser.dxf cuts the same card in nominal 6 mm MDF. DXF is finished geometry
in mm, with closed CUT contours. Measure actual stock and apply measured kerf once in CAM.
Use receiver_map.svg to mark the labels by hand.

SCREW REFERENCE: one M3 x 20 countersunk screw + one M3 nut (not included). Side-load
the nut into the beam; insert adapter from outside into the SAME 6.0 x 4.6 hole; beam
seats inside. Adapter prints countersink DOWN, cheeks UP. Nominal flange depth is 2.4 mm.

Prototype faces use this same nominal hole; printed stops stay angled, MDF stops solid
through 6 mm. Full-length V7 rails remain unselected. Physical insertion force, durability,
MDF wear and loaded performance are unverified; CAD flex probes are not force simulations.
