Version 4 modular drawer unit. All dimensions are millimetres.
3dpr.scad: complete kit overview, all components on Z=0; it may exceed one bed.
front.scad: print ONE front plate with its catch lip. face_plate.scad is the front alias.
back.scad: print ONE rear plate without a catch lip; do not print two front plates.
rail.scad: universal shelf/top/top-corner rail. bottom_rail.scad: reinforced bottom row.
rails_plate_*.scad: bed-sized batches containing the exact rail quantities in assembly.json.
assembled.scad: reference only. Face-fit 3dpr contains one front plate; back.scad is an optional rear fit test.
Print rails on their long flat bearing faces, as generated. No standing long rails.
Load M3 nuts sideways into both end pockets before fitting the second face plate.
Seat the 3 mm pointed keys, then use M3 x 16 countersunk screws from the outside.
Small horizontal screw bores and nut-slot roofs need short bridging; inspect the slicer.
Trial joint_fit and face_fit first. Tray clearance, fit and loading need physical checks.
