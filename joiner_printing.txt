V8 JOINER ACCESSORY FOR 1x1 AND 2x2 FRAMES | M3 x 25 countersunk screws and standard M3 hex nuts
Print joiners.stl and joiner_backers.stl once per set. Each file includes a two-hole strip and a four-hole plate. The holes are on a 10 mm pitch for touching 6 mm faces.
Print all parts as supplied: outside faces and nut-pocket mouths down. Clear the bores and ensure the nuts drop into the rear pockets by hand.
Use joiners.stl with either upper push-in caps or screw adapters. Both fronts are 8.4 mm tall. Their underside pockets bridge the fitted 4.6 mm caps with 0.8 mm overhead and 0.6 mm around the sides. For a looser fit, use joiners_clasp_clearance.stl (1.1 mm overhead and 0.9 mm around the sides). Print only one front style per junction.
To update existing V8 joiners, reprint the outside fronts only. The deeper head recess seats the existing M3 x 25 countersunk screws at their original position. Reuse the V8 backers, frame parts and nuts.
Fit the inside backers while there is access to the seams. Before closing the frame, load nuts into the backing piece from its open rear face. Hold them in place. Approach the inside face with the backer 8 mm above its installed position; slide down into the rail relief. Hold the backer against the inside face while starting screws from the outside.
Seat the push-in caps or screw adapters first. Bring the joiner straight onto the faceplate, with the pockets over the fittings and the seating lands against the panel. Remove the front to withdraw a cap it overlaps. At the rear, turn the outside plate over about Y and turn the inside backer 180 degrees about Z relative to its print orientation. Turn vertical strips +90 degrees on the right edge and -90 on the left as seen in front CAD coordinates. Match the reliefs to the runners before tightening.
Use two screws and nuts per strip or four per corner. Seat gently and confirm the drawer and clasps move freely. Use an open frame or remove drawers for access.
FIT SAMPLE: print joiner_fit_samples.stl, your selected front style and joiner_backers.stl. The sample contains two separate top face corners, one shortened screw beam, one shortened snap beam, a screw adapter, the full-square friction cap and a printed stop section. Arrange the straight outer edges of the two face corners together with the corner bores 10 mm apart. Mount the screw end and adapter on one corner and the snap end and push-in cap on the other. Use the two-hole joiner and backer across the seam. Use the normal M3 x 20 beam fixing for the screw beam, and two M3 x 25 fixings for the strip. Check seating, nut capture, hand insertion and latch operation before printing a full batch.
The joiners_clip_clearance.png section shows the fitted clip beneath the standard front. Only the test print can confirm tolerances on your printer.
