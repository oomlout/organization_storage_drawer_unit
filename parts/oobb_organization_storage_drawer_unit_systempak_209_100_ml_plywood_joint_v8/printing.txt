Use the single-contact plate at an outside corner. Use the two-contact plate where two stacked frames meet the same plywood side. Both 3 mm plates finish flush with the outside edge of the plywood. The 3.5 × 16 mm countersunk wood screw is centred 6 mm from either plywood face.
Remove the screw and adapter at each participating corner. Seat one pair of guide cheeks in each exposed beam receiver. Refit each M3 screw into its captive beam nut. Mark the plywood through the other hole, drill a Ø2.5 mm pilot 16 mm deep in the centre of the edge, then fit the wood screw. Test the pilot in an offcut.
