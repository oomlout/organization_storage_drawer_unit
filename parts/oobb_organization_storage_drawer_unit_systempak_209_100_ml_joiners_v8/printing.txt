Join adjacent modules using the M3 × 20 countersunk screws and captive nuts already in their outer corner beams. Fit a bracket at each shared junction on both faces.
Remove the individual adapters and screws from the corners being joined. Leave each nut in its beam. Press the bracket guides into those receivers and reinstall the same screws. For a two-hole upright, point the narrow spine toward the outside edge. Rotate the bracket in its own plane to fit the opposite side. Use the across strip for a left-to-right seam and a four-hole bracket where four frames meet. On a 2x7 below a vertical joint, centre its first two fixed location labels 9.65 and 20.85 mm below the top corner beam axis.
