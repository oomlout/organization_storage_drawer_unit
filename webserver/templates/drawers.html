How the comparison works

Printed prices combine filament at 1p/g with one lidless pot per drawer. Frame mass is estimated from solid CAD volume at 1.24 g/cm³; sliced prints can differ. Pot cost allocates a 500-piece carton including VAT and unused lids. Both Systempak pot sizes were on backorder when checked. Delivery, electricity, labour, print waste and assembly hardware are excluded.

Cost per kg uses the empty cabinet, or the estimated printed frame plus pots. Unknown product weights remain blank; unconfirmed retailer weights are provisional. Total litres and £/litre use the rounded drawer capacities, which may be nominal or estimates from dimensions. Fit/layout samples and separate front/back halves are hidden by default because they are not complete storage units. Separate halves show filament cost without pots.

Supplier photos belong to their respective owners. Printed images show the SCAD frame without pots. Printed dimensions exclude removable ears; the material estimate includes them.
