This durable storage cabinet from Treston has 60 drawers made from crystal clear polystyrene. Combining a stylish design with practical accessories to provide flexible and efficient small parts storage, this cabinet can be free-standing or wall-mounted and is ideal for use in almost any industrial environment.


  • Drawer size 175 x 55 x 37mm
  • Polypropylene frame and galvanised steel shelves
  • Overall size 550 x 310 x 180mm
  • Dark grey colour
  • Self adhesive labels provided for each drawer
  • Drawer handles recessed within frame for additional protection during transport
